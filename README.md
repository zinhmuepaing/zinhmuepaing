## 🏆 Featured: Gym Dolphin

### Ryan, the AI voice agent that won AI-GNITION 2026

<a href="https://youtu.be/JYU1Y21LyjM">
  <img src="https://img.youtube.com/vi/JYU1Y21LyjM/maxresdefault.jpg" alt="Watch the Gym Dolphin demo" width="640"/>
</a>

**[▶ Watch the demo](https://youtu.be/JYU1Y21LyjM)** · **[View the source](https://github.com/zinhmuepaing/gym-dolphin)**

- **Champion, AI-GNITION 2026** (200+ participants), Problem Statement by Eslitec. I led a team of 4 and owned the full technical implementation.
- Ryan phones gym members who are drifting away, finds out why, and pitches like a real salesperson without the hard sell: one offer that fits their reason, dropped after a no.
- **Code decides, the model speaks.** A pure-Python decision engine picks every allowed offer and its cost. Claude only works out what the member means and how to say it. Staff watch every step live and can take over.
- Backed by **479 tests**. Built with FastAPI, React, ElevenLabs voice, Supabase, and Claude.
